Every overpass is a bridge. But not every bridge is an overpass. This is a bridge. This is an overpass. Which is which? A bridge crosses water or a valley. An overpass crosses another road. Trivia: the longest bridge, in China, is about 165 kilometers! This is a tunnel. This is an underpass. Spot the difference? A tunnel goes through a hill, or under water. An underpass is a short way under a road. Trivia: the longest train tunnel runs 57 kilometers under the Alps. My trick: over the road, overpass. Under the road, underpass.
