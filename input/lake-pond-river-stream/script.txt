Lake or pond? Most people just guess. This is a lake. This is a pond. What's the difference? A lake is deep, so its bottom stays dark. A pond is shallow. Sun hits the whole bottom. But Walden Pond is over 100 feet deep. So it's really a lake! This is a river. This is a stream. So where's the line? A river is big, fed by smaller streams. A stream is small and shallow. The Mississippi starts as a stream you can walk across. Then it runs over 2,300 miles! Tip: a river is just a stream that grew up. Like and follow for more!
