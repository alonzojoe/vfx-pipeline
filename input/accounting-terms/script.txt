Say my shop makes $10,000. Why do I only keep $3,000? This is gross profit. And this is net profit. What's the difference? Gross profit is what's left after subtracting the cost of making or buying what you sell. Net profit is what's left after ALL business expenses. Now, accounts payable versus accounts receivable! Accounts payable is money your business owes. Accounts receivable is money customers owe your business. Remember: Payable means you pay. Receivable means you receive! Learned something?
